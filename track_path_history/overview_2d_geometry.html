Notes

  • All pallets travel clockwise: top rail (6) left-to-right, right connector (1) down, bottom rail (3) right-to-left, left connector (5) up.
  • Path 2 and Path 4 are mold spurs that branch off the right and left connectors.
  • Cleanout is at the left junction (Path 5 start); HOME / Cold Start is on the bottom rail.
