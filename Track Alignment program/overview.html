MagneMotion Track — Operator Overview

This is the labeled track reference built in the Track Alignment program. It shows the 3-D track photo with station labels and the calibrated PLC path colors.

Direction of travel

Pallets travel clockwise around the loop: top rail (6) left-to-right, right connector (1) downward, lower rail (3) right-to-left, left connector (5) upward. Mold spurs (2 and 4) branch off the right and left connectors respectively.
